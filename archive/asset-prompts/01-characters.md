# 01 — Character sheets

Make each character first as a **single hero portrait**. Iterate until the face is right, then use that portrait as the reference for the **turnaround** and **expressions** sheets. Plain light-gray background for all of them.

Add the **style block** from `00-style.md` to every prompt.

---

## Boss — "Pak Hendra" (working name)

### Portrait (approve this first)
```
Full-body character portrait of an Indonesian man in his mid-40s, warm friendly face, kind caring eyes, full well-groomed beard along the jaw and chin with a clean-shaven upper lip (no mustache), short neat black hair with a little gray at the temples, medium build, wearing a navy-and-cream batik long-sleeve shirt and dark trousers, brown leather shoes, standing relaxed with a gentle confident smile, plain light-gray studio background
```
> If the tool keeps adding a mustache: add "shaved upper lip, sunnah-style beard, no hair above the lip" and put "mustache" in the negative prompt.

### Turnaround sheet
```
Character turnaround sheet of the same Indonesian man (see reference): front view, three-quarter view, side view and back view, standing in a neutral pose, same face, same beard with no mustache, same navy-and-cream batik shirt and dark trousers, evenly spaced on a plain light-gray background, consistent proportions
```

### Expressions sheet
```
Expression sheet of the same Indonesian man (see reference), head and shoulders, 4 expressions in a 2x2 grid: concerned (brows slightly furrowed, looking on thoughtfully), curious (head tilted, slight smile), relieved (soft exhale, gentle smile), proud and happy (big warm smile, eyes crinkled), same beard with no mustache, same batik shirt, plain light-gray background
```
