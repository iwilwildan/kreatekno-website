# 03 — Key frames (one per beat)

These are the start and end frames for the video segments, and also the poster and fallback images. For every prompt:
- Attach the **character sheets** and the **office reference**.
- Add the **style block** from `00-style.md`.
- Generate the 16:9 and 9:16 versions separately.

**Composition rules**
- 16:9: keep the action in the **right two-thirds**; the left third stays calm (the headline goes there).
- 9:16: keep the action in the **lower 60%**; the top stays calm (the headline goes there).
- The camera **pulls back and rises** from beat 1 to beat 4, so each beat is wider than the one before.

---

## Beat 1 — Stuck in the grind (close)

**16:9**
```
Close medium shot in the office from the reference: the young Indonesian woman in a dusty-rose hijab sits at her wooden desk, surrounded by tall stacks of paper, sticky notes and an overflowing inbox tray, typing quickly on a laptop, looking tired and overwhelmed. Behind her, softly out of focus, the bearded Indonesian boss in a navy batik shirt stands watching with gentle concern. Slightly cool overcast daylight, a bit cluttered. Action in the right two-thirds of the frame, the left third is a calm blurred wall. 16:9
```

**9:16**
```
Vertical close medium shot: the young Indonesian woman in a dusty-rose hijab at her desk in the lower part of the frame, surrounded by tall paper stacks and sticky notes, typing on a laptop, tired and overwhelmed. Above and behind her, softly out of focus, the bearded boss in a navy batik shirt watches with gentle concern. Slightly cool daylight. The top 40% of the frame is a calm, softly blurred office wall. 9:16
```

## Beat 2 — A helper arrives

**16:9**
```
Same framing as the previous image, camera pulled back very slightly: a small soft glowing orb of warm-white and teal light, the size of a tennis ball, floats beside the woman's monitor. The woman in the dusty-rose hijab has stopped typing and leans back, looking at it with curiosity and a small surprised smile. The paper stacks on her desk look a little tidier. The bearded boss behind her, still softly out of focus, tilts his head with interest. Neutral daylight with a soft glow from the orb. Action in the right two-thirds. 16:9
```

**9:16**
```
Vertical framing, camera pulled back slightly: a small soft glowing orb of warm-white and teal light floats beside the monitor of the woman in the dusty-rose hijab, who leans back curious with a small surprised smile. The desk is a little tidier. The bearded boss behind her tilts his head with interest. Neutral daylight. Action in the lower 60%, calm upper area. 9:16
```

## Beat 3 — Moving up

**16:9**
```
Wider medium shot of the same office: the woman in the dusty-rose hijab now stands at the whiteboard holding her notebook, pointing at a simple sketched diagram (abstract shapes and arrows only, no words) while the young man with glasses in a light-blue shirt listens and adds to it with a marker, both engaged and smiling. Her desk is now tidy, with the small glowing orb resting quietly beside the monitor. In the mid-ground the bearded boss in the navy batik shirt watches them and begins to smile. Warming sunlight starts to come through the windows. Action in the right two-thirds. 16:9
```

**9:16**
```
Vertical wider shot: in the lower part of the frame, the woman in the dusty-rose hijab at the whiteboard pointing at an abstract diagram (no words) with the young man with glasses in a light-blue shirt, both engaged and smiling; her tidy desk with the small glowing orb nearby; the bearded boss in a navy batik shirt watching them and starting to smile. Warming sunlight. Calm upper area. 9:16
```

## Beat 4 — Growing together (wide, end frame)

**16:9**
```
Wide shot of the same office, camera higher and further back: the bearded Indonesian boss in a navy batik shirt has joined the woman in the dusty-rose hijab and the young man with glasses at the whiteboard and meeting table, one hand resting kindly on the woman's shoulder, all three smiling and talking together, relaxed and proud. The office is tidy, lively and full of plants, with the small glowing orb barely visible on the desk. Warm golden-hour sunlight streaming through the windows. Group in the right two-thirds, the left third is calm. 16:9
```

**9:16**
```
Vertical wide shot, camera higher and further back: the bearded boss in a navy batik shirt with a hand on the shoulder of the woman in the dusty-rose hijab, the young man with glasses beside them, all three smiling and talking at the whiteboard, warm golden-hour light, tidy lively office. The group fills the lower 60% with faces clearly readable, calm upper area. 9:16
```
